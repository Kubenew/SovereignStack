### We've Built an Open, Verifiable Governance Architecture for Advanced Autonomous Systems

While the world debates AI safety in white papers, SovereignStack has codified it into verifiable, enforceable protocols:

**Human Override That Cannot Be Ignored**
The `human://` and `override://` protocols bind human authority to biometric identity. An air‑gapped, hardware‑enforced kill‑switch cuts power to AI accelerators in under 100 milliseconds. No software can block it. No agent can negotiate with it. It is the final safeguard — and it is now a certified, testable standard.

**Agents That Explain Their Own Thinking — While They Think**
RFC‑0056 turns inference‑time search (MCTS, beam search, agentic retrieval) into auditable `search://` objects. Every step, every evidence source, every decision path is cryptographically signed and replayable. Black‑box reasoning becomes transparent governance.

**Self‑Modifying AI Under Continuous Oversight**
RFC‑0057 introduces `meta://` — a standard for agents to publish their own self‑model, log every self‑improvement, report alignment drift in real time, and prove they haven't crossed the line. If an AGI rewrites its own code, you'll know what changed, why, and whether it's still safe.

**Zero‑Knowledge Proofs in Hardware Enclaves**
SovereignStack now runs alignment verification inside AWS Nitro Enclaves, Intel SGX, and AMD SEV‑SNP. The host OS can't see it. The cloud provider can't touch it. Even if the entire software stack is compromised, the ZK‑proof verifier remains trustworthy — and it can trigger the kill‑switch on its own authority.
